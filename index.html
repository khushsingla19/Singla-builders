Complete Commercial Hub & Infrastructure Ecosystem

Singla Builders is currently executing the full turn-key master construction of a premier 6-acre commercial and industrial hub for Hand Group. The project encompasses heavy civil structures, civil underground networks and modern commercial architecture:

Commercial Showrooms

Wide frontal commercial architecture with reinforced concrete frames and high-load capacity floors.

Water Tank & Sewage Treatment Plant

High-capacity underground/overhead water storage reservoirs integrated with an advanced on-site sewage treatment plant (STP) for sustainable wastewater recycling.

Engineered Parking Area

Heavy interlocking and concrete pavement grids built for continuous heavy vehicular movement.

Rainwater Harvesting Pits

Eco-compliant multi-chamber filtration recharge pits ensuring groundwater sustainability.

Deep Sewer Line Network

Precision-graded underground sewer and stormwater lines engineered for zero waterlogging.

Internal Arterial Roads & Access

Graded road network with sub-base consolidation and heavy asphalt/concrete surfacing.
